COMMON QUESTIONS

A few things people ask before reaching out.

How much does mediation cost, and who pays?

Mediation is $150 CAD per hour, plus applicable taxes. Unless otherwise agreed, the participant who initiates the mediation is responsible for payment, due within 24 hours of each session by e-transfer or cheque.

Is mediation legally binding?

Mediation itself does not impose a legally binding outcome. If the participants reach an agreement, the terms can be documented in a Memorandum of Understanding. The MOU records what the participants have agreed to; it is not a decision imposed by the mediator, and it is not drafted or reviewed as a legal instrument. Participants may choose to have the agreement reviewed by independent legal counsel.

Do I need a lawyer?

No, a lawyer is not required to participate in mediation. However, participants may wish to obtain independent legal advice before signing an agreement, particularly where legal rights or obligations are involved.
