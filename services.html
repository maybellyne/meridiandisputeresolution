HOW WE HELP

A better way to navigate disputes.

Meridian Dispute Resolution Inc. provides structured, neutral dispute resolution services for individuals, families, workplaces, businesses, and communities. We help people address conflict constructively, understand their options, and work toward practical outcomes.

HOW WE HELP

Dispute resolution for a range of situations.

Family & Separation

Mediation for separation and family matters where decisions need to be made about parenting, property, support, or other arrangements. A neutral setting gives people an opportunity to work through disagreements and make informed decisions together.

Estate & Elder Matters

Mediation for disagreements involving estates, inheritances, caregiving, family responsibilities, or decisions affecting an older family member. A neutral conversation can help clarify competing interests and develop practical arrangements.

Workplace

Mediation for workplace disagreements involving communication, working relationships, responsibilities, performance concerns or other conflicts between employees, managers or colleagues. The focus is on addressing the issues directly and determining whether a workable resolution is possible.

Business & Commercial

Mediation for business disputes involving partners, owners, customers, contractors, suppliers, or other commercial relationships. Mediation helps clarify the issues, explore options and work toward an agreement while preserving business relationships where appropriate.

Neighbour & Community

Mediation for disputes between neighbours or within communities involving property, noise, boundaries, shared responsibilities, or ongoing disagreements. The purpose is to provide a neutral setting to address the issues and develop practical arrangements for moving forward.

THE MERIDIAN APPROACH

A process built around the people involved.

Mediation provides a structured, neutral, and confidential way to address conflict, have meaningful conversations, and work through issues with the support of a neutral third party. The process is voluntary, flexible and collaborative, allowing the parties to communicate constructively, and remain involved in shaping an outcome they can reach and accept.
