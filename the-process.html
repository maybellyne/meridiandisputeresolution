HOW MEDIATION WORKS

How the process works.

Mediation is a structured process designed to give people a meaningful opportunity to talk through a dispute, understand what matters, consider their options, and work toward a resolution they can reach and accept.

THE PROCESS

A structured approach from the first conversation through to resolution.

01

Free Initial Consultation

The initial consultation is a 30-minute conversation by phone or videoconferencing. It provides an opportunity to discuss the situation, ask questions, understand how mediation works, and assess whether mediation is appropriate. There is no obligation to proceed. If mediation is not suitable, that will be explained.

02

Preparation

Before mediation begins, participants receive information about the process, what to expect, and how to prepare. Relevant documents or information may be identified in advance, and participants have an opportunity to raise questions or concerns. Preparation helps ensure that everyone comes to the mediation ready to discuss the issues that need to be addressed.

03

Mediation Sessions

Sessions are facilitated by a neutral mediator who has no stake in the outcome. Depending on the dispute, sessions may be conducted jointly, separately (caucus format), or in a combination of both. The mediator guides discussion — not toward a predetermined answer, but toward an outcome the parties themselves can reach and accept.

04

Memorandum of Understanding

Where agreement is reached, Meridian documents the terms in a written memorandum of understanding (MOU). This is not a legal contract — it is a clear record of what was agreed. Parties are encouraged to have independent legal counsel review the document before signing. Where no agreement is reached, all legal rights are preserved.
